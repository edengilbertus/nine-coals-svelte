# Nine Coals direction

Source: https://saastemp-zeta.vercel.app, explicitly supplied by the user to recreate.

Reading this as an open-fire restaurant prototype for diners, in the reference's bold, smoky, editorial visual language. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.

- Near-black, ash, ember orange and flame amber reproduce the source's fire identity. Per-bed colors identify the current cooking heat, not unrelated decoration.
- Clash Display reproduces the monumental brand typography; General Sans provides legible body/UI copy; Instrument Serif italic is the source's warm editorial voice.
- Original smoke, grill photographs and food cutouts show the subject itself. They are reference prototype photography, not photographs of a real Nine Coals restaurant.
- Large stacked headlines, layered food imagery, asymmetric light editorial sections and dark chronological photo panels preserve the source composition and rhythm.
- Generous desktop whitespace separates editorial beats; tighter, reflowed phone layouts maintain content density and 44px controls.
- GSAP introduces the hero, reveals the story through scrolling and transitions one signature dish at a time. Reduced motion displays everything immediately.
- Native dialogs and visible focus keep the mobile menu, photo viewer and prototype-information controls usable with a keyboard.
- The fixed dark identity and deliberately light story sections come from the reference. A new theme toggle would alter it.
- The source is a sample brand. All transactional flows remain explicitly local demonstrations; no new claims, real availability or testimonials are invented.
- Shadows belong to the overlapping timeline, the floating food cutout and transient basket feedback, where they separate layers; editorial sections remain flat.
- The single blurred navigation surface keeps links readable while content scrolls behind it. Cards, forms and dialogs stay solid.
- Chevrons indicate previous/next or rotation, plus/minus changes quantity, and the labeled menu icon exposes navigation. None are decorative CTA arrows.
- Small fields, rounded photographs, native dialog surfaces and pill CTAs use different radii to distinguish editing, imagery and actions.
- A narrow dark outline separates the desktop hero lettering from the pale steak bone without relocating the reference's overlapping type and photograph.
